# 🎓 EDU Faculty Appointment Booking System

> A centralized web application designed to streamline the process of booking appointments between students and faculty members at East Delta University (EDU).

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Currently, students waste valuable time trying to find teachers due to unknown schedules, slow email replies, or simply waiting outside office doors. This causes frustration for both students and teachers. 

**The Solution:** The EDU Faculty Appointment Booking System solves this by providing a central digital platform. Faculties can post their available office hours and counseling times, while students can log in using their university accounts to view these live schedules and book meeting slots instantly from their devices.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Role-Based Dashboards:** Separate, secure, and easy-to-use interfaces for Students, Faculties, and Administrators.
- **Live Scheduling:** Faculty members can easily update, manage, and control their availability on an interactive calendar.
- **Instant Booking:** Students can search for faculties, view real-time availability, and request, view, or cancel appointments without any hassle.
- **Automated Management:** Eliminates the confusion of walk-in appointments and physical notice boards, saving time for everyone.
